Facebook lets chat participants customize conversation themes:

1 Open any conversation from your chat list.
2 Click the Contact Name or the ⓘ Info Icon at the top right of the chat header.
3 In the Chat Info sidebar, click "Change Theme" under Customize Chat.
4 Choose from preset themes, color gradients, or select "Create with AI" to generate a custom prompt-based theme!

💡 Tip for narrow sidebars: Click the Desktop View icon (◫) in the top toolbar to show the Chat Info and Theme Customization panel side-by-side with your messages!
