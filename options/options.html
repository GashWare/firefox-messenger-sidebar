🎨 Facebook Native Chat Theming & AI Themes

How to set shared chat themes & AI-generated backgrounds with other users

Step 1

Click on any conversation in your chat list to open it.

Step 2

Click the Contact Name or the ⓘ Info Icon at the top right of the conversation header.

Step 3

Under "Customize chat" in the right-hand panel, click "Change theme".

Step 4

Pick any preset theme, custom gradient, or select "Create with AI" to generate a custom AI chat theme!

💡 Desktop Mode tip: In narrow sidebars, click the Desktop 3-Column icon (◫) in the top toolbar to keep the Chat Info and Theme panel open side-by-side with your messages!
